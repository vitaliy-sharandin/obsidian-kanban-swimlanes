# Kanban Swimlanes 1.0.4

- Positioned linked-note hover previews next to the hovered card, including cards near viewport edges.
- Added expandable inline content and editing for both linked notes and regular cards.
- Added **Expand all** and **Collapse all** controls to the swimlane toolbar.
- Made expansion and editing more responsive with lightweight editors, debounced saves, and deferred board serialization.
- Improved drag-and-drop precision for compact and expanded cards with pointer-prioritized hit testing and refreshed layout measurements.
- Shortened drag animations and corrected vertical drop-target center calculations.
- Preserved custom preview dimensions when switching card display modes.
